# CodeSplash

> **Empowering innovation through inspiration from the timeless legacy of the pyramids.**

**CodeSplash** is a nationwide hackathon organized by the **Computer Science Students' Association (CSSA)** of the **Faculty of Computing and Technology (FCT), University of Kelaniya**, Sri Lanka.

---

## About CodeSplash

CodeSplash challenges university undergraduates and aspiring tech innovators across Sri Lanka to design, build, and deploy high-impact software solutions. Rooted in endurance, structure, and foundational engineering, the competition brings together cross-functional teams to solve real-world problems under competitive time constraints.

* **Organizer:** Computer Science Students' Association (CSSA)
* **Institution:** Faculty of Computing and Technology, University of Kelaniya
* **Official Website:** [codesplash.cssa.lk](https://codesplash.cssa.lk)

## ⚖️ Hackathon Repository Guidelines

For participants working inside this organization:

1. **Commit Early & Often:** Evaluation is based on repository activity and progression during the sprint period.
2. **Strict Submission Deadline:** Repositories will automatically freeze (read-only mode) when the final countdown ends. Late pushes or uncommitted local work cannot be considered.
3. **Integrity & Originality:** All code developed during the sprint must adhere to standard academic and industry integrity policies. Boilerplates and public libraries are allowed, but core project logic must be authored during the competition.
4. **Access Control:** Outside collaborators have write access strictly to their assigned team repository. Never expose team credentials, API tokens, or secrets.
